# Иллюстрация донатной печати

Инструмент: встроенный `image_gen`, режим generate, transparent_background=true. Финальный файл: `stream-tools/obs/app/assets/donation-seal-v1.webp` (960×720,197492байта). PNG-оригинал сохранён в рабочем каталоге; WebP подготовлен на сервере через sharp-cli. CSS делит один кэшируемый файл на две половины; сумма, ник, текст, аудиоиндикатор и анимации реализованы в коде.

## Финальный промпт

Create a polished cinematic game-stream donation alert ART ASSET, not a screenshot and not UI. A mysterious crimson seal opening into an elliptical void, premium dark fantasy craftsmanship. Wide landscape composition, transparent background AND genuinely transparent central hole. The main oval ring is made of richly textured deep red wax merging into folded crimson silk, a few dark polished obsidian fragments, tiny warm copper highlights. Two sculptural torn wax halves arranged left and right with a clean vertical split through top and bottom of ring, so a programmer can animate the two halves separating. Shape naturally asymmetric, physical and tactile, elegant rather than a generic neon sci-fi circle. A few fine red threads curve outward in graceful long arcs, restrained sparks near the edges. No face, no character, no playing cards, no suits, no dice, no coins, no text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no runes. Main ring centered, outside width 76 percent of image and outside height 70 percent; central empty transparent oval hole width 44 percent of image, height 32 percent. Maintain clean negative space inside for a large code-rendered donation amount. Entire object must fit safely with generous transparent margins, no cropped threads. Straight-on view with subtle 3D depth, dramatic grazing red light, crisp microtexture and luminous warm rims; no background glow rectangle, no floor, no frame, no drop shadow plane, no checkerboard. Palette dark garnet, lacquer red, warm pale copper. Finished production-ready transparent PNG asset suitable for a 560 by 440 transparent OBS alert. The full widget will place this ornament above the donor nickname/message; don't draw any of that UI.
